# Capture for good

OMNI reads what any browser already renders: computed styles, layout and
behavior. It's for learning from good interfaces and making yours better.

## Fine
- Auditing your own or your client's product
- Studying how a well-made site handles spacing, states and motion, then applying those *patterns* to your own design
- Extracting tokens from a product you own to build a design system
- Competitive UX reviews (findings, not copies)
- Accessibility audits to report or fix problems

## Not fine
- Cloning a site's brand, logo, illustrations, photos, fonts you haven't licensed, or copy, and presenting it as yours
- Building look-alike pages to impersonate a company (phishing, fake stores)
- Capturing pages behind logins you aren't authorized to use
- Hammering sites: OMNI loads one page per command; don't loop it against someone else's server

## Practice
- Check the site's terms and `robots.txt` before capturing third-party pages.
- Keep captured screenshots private unless you have rights to share them.
- When rebuilding "inspired by" work, swap in your own brand colors, type, imagery and words. Keep the craft, not the identity.
