# Landing / marketing page

**Core tasks:** understand what it is in 5 s · trust it · take one action.

**Must exist**
- Brand obvious in the first viewport (OpenAI's test: remove the nav — could this belong to another brand? If yes, redo).
- Real product imagery/screens, real numbers with sources, one primary CTA.
- Fast LCP; images sized; works at 390 px.

**Slop risks (highest of all surfaces):** centered hero + 3 feature cards, indigo/purple gradients, gradient headline text, aurora glow, fake logos and testimonials, round-number stats, em-dash slogans.

**Concepts & themes**
- Brand-led: generate a preset from the brand's hue (`omni themegen` with your own JSON), then follow the direction doc. Don't reuse tool themes for marketing.

**Astryx:** `centered-hero`, `gallery-hero`. Caution: a centered hero is itself a top slop tell; use the template for structure and get the identity from DIRECTION.md, or pick a non-centered composition.
